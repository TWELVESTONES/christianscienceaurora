# Launch Gate

Do not launch until the following are verified and approved:

- Formal name, address, phone, domain email, service times, service duration
- Parking, entrance, accessibility, weather closure, and Sunday School check-in
- Reading Room location, hours, phone, inventory, fulfillment, and refund rules
- Remote attendance and public access policy
- Giving provider, funds, receipts, tax language, privacy, and refunds
- Product covers, sermon media, official text, quotations, trademarks, lecture media, and archival material
- Youth releases, child-safety rules, metadata stripping, and editorial safeguards
- Forms, email delivery, spam protection, retention, deletion, privacy, and legal terms
- Redirects, canonical URLs, sitemaps, structured data, search-console ownership
- WCAG 2.2 AA review, keyboard flows, captions, transcripts, accessible downloads
- Build, lint, type-check, unit, integration, accessibility, and critical-path tests
