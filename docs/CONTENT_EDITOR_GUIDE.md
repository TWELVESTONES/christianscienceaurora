# Content Editor Guide

- Lead with welcome and practical information.
- Use short paragraphs, descriptive headings, and direct actions.
- Explain Lesson-Sermon, Reader, testimony meeting, practitioner, and unfamiliar theological terms.
- Do not promise medical or physical outcomes.
- Do not copy official material merely because it is online. Link unless permission is documented.
- Every event needs an owner, status, date/time/time zone, location, accessibility, parking, publish date, expiration date, and archive behavior.
- Every image needs source, license, release status, alt text, and review date.
- Never publish child surnames, profiles, comments, location metadata, or recognizable images without documented guardian authorization.
- Review service times and contact details monthly; external resources semiannually; stale articles annually.
